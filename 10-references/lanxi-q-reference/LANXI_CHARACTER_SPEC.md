# Lanxi Q Character Spec

## Core Identity

- Name: Lanxi.
- Style: clean modern chibi anime desktop pet, cute but cool, sprite-friendly.
- Personality: sweet, gentle, lively, slightly hip-hop/street-dance confident.
- Palette: light aqua-blue hair, white school-uniform base, black and pale-pink sporty jacket accents, silver accessories.

## Fixed Visual Traits

- Very long light aqua-blue hair, simplified into readable soft shapes.
- Silver-gray eyes.
- Silver J hair clip.
- Silver H letter earring on the left ear.
- White school-uniform outfit with sleeved white shirt/blouse and pale pink tie accents.
- Oversized sporty street-dance jacket in white, black, and pale pink.
- White pleated skirt.
- Clean black belt.
- Simplified silver waist chain with a small silver X charm.
- White over-the-knee socks.
- Black academy-style shoes.

## Motion And Sprite Rules

- Keep the full body visible and centered in every frame.
- Leave generous padding on all sides, especially around long hair.
- Hair may sway, but it must stay inside its own frame and never enter a neighboring frame.
- Avoid wide flying hair, extreme stride, motion blur, speed lines, dust, shadows, floating effects, or cropped shoes/hair.
- For 192x208 cells, prefer compact poses around 75-85% of the cell height.

## Skirt And Leg Safety Rules

- Running and jumping states must include white safety shorts / bloomers / pettipants under the skirt.
- The safety shorts should be about the same visual length as the skirt or slightly shorter, subtle and clean, matching the white outfit.
- The skirt hem, safety shorts, socks, and legs must read as separate clean shapes.
- Avoid awkward thigh deformation, tangled upper thighs, or any X-shaped/fork-like crossing between the legs.
- The silver X waist charm must hang from the belt or waist chain in front of the skirt or slightly to one side. It must not fall between the thighs or be mistaken for leg anatomy.
